CloudCart Customer Policy Handbook

Section 1: Returns and Refunds
We accept returns within 30 days of purchase, as long as the item is unused and in its
original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the return is
received. Sale items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned. Customers are responsible
for return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

Section 2: Shipping
Standard shipping takes 4-6 business days within India and is free for orders over
Rs. 999. Express shipping costs Rs. 199 and delivers within 2 business days. We do not
currently ship internationally. Orders placed before 2 PM IST ship the same day.

Section 3: Warranty
All electronics purchased from CloudCart come with a 12-month manufacturer warranty
covering defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental
damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs. To claim warranty service, customers
must provide the original invoice.

Section 4: Payment Methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking. We do not accept cash on
delivery for orders above Rs. 10,000. All payments are processed securely through
Razorpay, and CloudCart never stores full card numbers on its own servers.

Section 5: Account and Privacy
Customers can create an account using their email or phone number. We retain order
history and account data for 3 years after the last activity, after which it is
automatically deleted upon request. Customers can request full account deletion by
emailing privacy@cloudcart.example. We do not sell customer data to third parties.

Section 6: Subscription Plans
CloudCart Plus is a subscription costing Rs. 499 per year, offering free express
shipping on all orders and early access to sales. Subscribers can cancel anytime from
their account settings; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing
cycle, and no partial refunds are given for early cancellation.

Section 7: Customer Support
Customer support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM IST, via chat, email,
and phone. The average first-response time is under 2 hours during business hours.
Support is not available on national holidays.

Section 8: Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of placing them. After that, if
the order has not yet shipped, a cancellation fee of Rs. 50 applies. Once an order has
shipped, it cannot be cancelled and must instead be returned after delivery.

Section 9: Loyalty Program
The CloudCart Rewards program gives customers 1 point for every Rs. 100 spent. Points
can be redeemed at checkout at a rate of Rs. 1 per point. Points expire 12 months after
they are earned if unused.

Section 10: Damaged or Incorrect Items
If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, customers must report it within 48 hours of
delivery with photo evidence. CloudCart will then arrange a free replacement or full
refund, whichever the customer prefers, without requiring the item to be shipped back
in the case of damaged goods.
